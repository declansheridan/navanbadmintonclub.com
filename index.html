    Navan-Badminton-Club badminton community volunteers youth-sport coaching

    Sunday Success: Inter League Finals for Grade 2 & Grade 7

    Badminton Inter-League Leinster-CAC Navan Meath

    What a Sunday of Leinster CAC Inter League action for our club!

    Grade 2 (Anneke, Kyle, Elaine, Alo, Aimee, Gerard & Miriam) booked their place in the Inter League Final after a brilliant 4–3 semi-final win over Carlow LTC — a real nail-biter that went right to the wire. In the final vs Portlaoise, it was tight through the first four games, but Portlaoise edged the mixed to take the title. So close, but an incredible run and a performance to be proud of. Well done all!

    Grade 7 (Robins, Anne, Lakshmi & Akhil) took on Carlow LTC in their Inter League Final after qualifying midweek — and they pushed them every step of the way. A 2–1 loss in games, but the sets tell the story: 6–4 overall. Every match was a battle and the fight on show was outstanding. Brilliant effort — and a Leinster medal to show for it. Fully deserved!

    A huge thank you to everyone who came out to support. The standard of badminton from our members in Terenure was something to be really proud of — a fantastic way to wrap up the competitive season.
