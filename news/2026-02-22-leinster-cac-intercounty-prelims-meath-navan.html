Leinster CAC Intercounty Prelims – Meath & Navan Players in Action (22nd February)

Authors
  • Name

    Twitter

The prelims continue this weekend in Kildalkey (Meath), Baldoyle (Dublin) and Roundwood (Wicklow).

Almost three weeks ago, the Meath Grade 2A, 2B, 4A and 6A panels booked their places at Finals Day in March — a fantastic achievement. Now it’s over to the Grade 3, 5A and 5B teams to make it a clean sweep for Meath!

We’re proud to have Navan representation across all three grades:

Grade 3:
Brenda Bradley, Elaine Doherty, Esther Mulvihill, Kyle Dunphy, Rachel McEntee, Sarah O’Halloran, Yashaswi Nalawade

Grade 5A:
Ciara Boylan, Clare Vaughan, James Walsh, Kevin Stewart, Mark Proctor

Grade 5B:
Clive Stone, Kirthi Reddy Kotha

Best of luck to all Meath panels and players this Sunday

An Mhí Abú! 💚💛
