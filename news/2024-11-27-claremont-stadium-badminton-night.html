Come to Claremont Stadium tonight for some exciting matches.

Matches Tonight:

🟣 GRADE 4 LADIES: Navan 'B' 🆚 Skryne 'A'
(Start 8:00pm)
Our Ladies 'B' will try to keep their winning streak, while Skryne aims to surprise. It’s going to be a thrilling match!

🟢 GRADE 7 MIXED: Navan 'B' 🆚 Oldcastle 'A'
(Start 8:00pm)
Can our Mixed 'B' continue their success, or will Oldcastle take the lead? Expect exciting twists!

🎷 Freestyle Play
From 8:30pm, 2 COURTS will be open for freestyle play. Show off your moves and enjoy the game!

🎸 Juvenile Club
Juvenile Club starts at 6:30pm for league match players only. This is your chance to play and improve!

Bring your best game and join us for a fun-filled evening. Don't miss out on the action! 🎉
