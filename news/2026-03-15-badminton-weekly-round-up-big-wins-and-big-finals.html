Badminton Weekly Round-Up: Big Wins and Big Finals

Authors
  • Name
    NBC
    Twitter

Another great week of badminton behind us — even with some tough results along the way.

A huge shoutout to our Grade 7 Ladies who lit up the week with a brilliant 4–0 win over Ardee — well done, ladies!
And once again, a massive congratulations to our Grade 2 Mixed ‘A’ on finishing runners-up in their Mixed League Final — a super achievement!

With the season reaching its climax, we’ve got loads of league and cup games coming up this week. Let’s push to get as many Men’s and Ladies teams into the playoff stages — and hopefully a few cup finals too.

Best of luck to everyone pulling on the Navan shirt! 🏸
