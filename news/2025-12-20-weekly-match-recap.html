Weekly Match Recap

Authors
  • Name
    NBC
    Twitter
    @NavanBC

What a super week of results with wins all over the place!

  • Monday: Our Grade 2 Men's team kicked things off with a draw against Ardee, while our Grade 6 Ladies 'A' team impressively defeated Athboy/Kells.

  • Tuesday: The Grade 7 Mixed team traveled to WSBC 'D' and came home with a brilliant 3-1 win.

  • Wednesday: Our Grade 5 Men's team battled to a 2-2 draw with Trim 'A', and the Grade 7 Men's team took down Skryne 'A' in style.

  • Thursday: Another victory as our Grade 4 Men's team beat Loughegar away. Our Grade 7 Ladies faced a tough challenge against WSBC 'A' but gave it everything they had.

As we wind down for Christmas, we have just one last match for 2025. Our Grade 2 Mixed 'A' team heads to Kildalkey on Monday night. Let's finish the year strong!
