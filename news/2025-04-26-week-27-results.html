Week 27 Results

If winning matches was an Olympic sport, Navan Badminton club would be heading to Los Angeles! Lots of excitement, big wins, and some losses—but that’s what makes it fun!

🏸 Monday, 21st April 2025

🟣 GRADE 3 LADIES: Skryne 1-7 Navan - CUP SF

Quick Review: Our Grade 3 Ladies played strong, beating Skryne 7-1 to reach the Cup Final. Great job!

🏸 Tuesday, 22nd April 2025

🔵 GRADE 5 MENS: Julianstown 4-7 Navan 'B' - CUP SF

Quick Review: Our Grade 5 Mens 'B' team won 7-4 against Julianstown in the Cup Semi-final. Another final spot secured!

🏸 Thursday, 24th April 2025

🔵 GRADE 4 MENS: Ardee 'A' 5-7 Navan - CUP SF

Quick Review: The Grade 4 Mens team had a hard-fought win against Ardee 'A', finishing 7-5. A well-earned spot in the Cup Final!

🏸 Friday, 25th April 2025

🟢 SENIOR MIXED: Navan 'A' 6-6 (Points: 203-226) Athboy/Kells 'B' - CUP FINAL

🟢 GRADE 4 MIXED: Navan 'A' 3-9 Boyne - CUP FINAL

Quick Review: Finals Friday was tough. Our Senior Mixed 'A' team ended 6-6 with Athboy/Kells 'B' but lost on points. The Grade 4 Mixed 'A' team faced a strong Boyne, finishing as runners-up. Reaching the final is a big achievement!

3 finalists confirmed this week and 2 runners-up. Proud Navan hearts all around. On to the final week of the MBA season—let’s go! 💪
