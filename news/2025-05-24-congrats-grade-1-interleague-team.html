Congrats, Grade 1 Interleague Team!

Authors
  • Name
    NBC
    Twitter
    @NavanBC

A big shoutout to our Grade 1 Interleague team for getting the runners-up medal at today's Badminton Ireland Grade C Final! 🥈 It wasn't our day for winning, but we're still proud.

Well done to Carrigaline — a strong team and well-deserved winners. Great games and lots to learn for next season. 🙌

This was our 233rd and last match of the season across Meath League, Meath Cup, and Interleague — what a season it's been. Thanks to our players, supporters, and everyone who kept the Navan spirit going. 💪

Take a break, recharge… we'll be back next season for more! 🔄
