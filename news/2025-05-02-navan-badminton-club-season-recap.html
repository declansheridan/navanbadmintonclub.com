Navan Badminton Club Season Recap

Authors
  • Name
    NBC
    Twitter
    @NavanBC

The Meath Badminton season has ended, and Navan shined with 3 Cup wins and 3 Runner-up finishes, along with our 3 Mixed Leagues, 3 Ladies Leagues, and 1 Men's League. Not bad, right?

Our Finals Week:

🥇 Grade 3 Ladies – CUP WINNERS!

Monday was big! The girls played great and won against Athboy/Kells. Aimee, Yash, Brenda, and Sarah were on point. They deserved that cup and their league win!

🥇 Grade 4 Mens – CUP WINNERS!

Kirthi, Kevin, Mark, and Dermot had one aim on Thursday in Kildalkey — to win. They did just that! WSBC was tough, but our guys pulled through.

🥇 Grade 7 Ladies – CUP WINNERS!

Claremont was alive on Wednesday with Theresa, Leeann, Dawn, and Karen beating Skryne 'A'. They worked well together and finished the season with a league and cup double!

🥈 Senior/Grade 2 Mixed 'A' – Runner-Up

A tight match in Kildalkey on Friday. Willie, Anneke, Lynn, and Paddy ended in a 6-6 draw against Athboy/Kells 'B'. They gave it their best!

🥈 Grade 4 Mixed 'A' – Runner-Up

Friday night was tough in Trim. Dermot, Rachel, Clare, and Mark played hard against Boyne. They showed great spirit!

🥈 Grade 5 Mens 'B' – Runner-Up

Tuesday in Athboy, David, Mark, Kirthi, and Clive gave it all against Ardee 'B'. They fought till the end and showed how much they've grown.

Big thanks to everyone who supported us — players, coaches, families, and fans. It's been a great season. Now, let's celebrate at our Club Awards Night on Saturday! 🥂
