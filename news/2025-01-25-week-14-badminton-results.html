Week 14 Badminton Results

Another week of badminton action. Here's a quick look at how our teams did.

🏸 Sunday, 19th January 2025

🔵 GRADE 7 MENS: Drumree 8-0 Navan 'A' - CUP

Summary: Tough start for Grade 7 Mens ‘A' with an 8-0 loss to Drumree in the Cup.

🏸 Monday, 20th January 2025

🟢 GRADE 3 MIXED: Navan 'B' 2-2 Loughegar

🟢 GRADE 4 MIXED: Navan 'B' 1-3 Mullingar

🔵 GRADE 6 MENS: Navan 4-8 Kildalkey - CUP

🟢 GRADE 7 MIXED: Oldcastle 'A' 4-0 Navan 'B'

Summary: Grade 3 Mixed ‘B' drew 2-2 with Loughegar. Grade 4 Mixed 'B' lost 3-1 to Mullingar. Grade 6 Mens lost 8-4 to Kildalkey in the Cup. Grade 7 Mixed 'B' faced a 4-0 defeat to Oldcastle 'A'.

🏸 Tuesday, 21st January 2025

🟢 SENIOR MIXED: Athboy/Kells 3-1 Navan 'B'

🟢 GRADE 3 MIXED: Mullingar 'A' 3-1 Navan 'B'

🔵 GRADE 5 MENS: WSBC 1-3 Navan 'B'

Summary: Grade 5 Mens 'B' won 3-1 over WSBC. Senior Mixed 'B' and Grade 3 Mixed 'B' both lost 3-1 to Athboy/Kells and Mullingar 'A'.

🏸 Wednesday, 22nd January 2025

🔵 GRADE 4 MENS: Navan 1-3 Ardee 'A'

🔵 GRADE 5 MENS: Boyne 2-2 Navan 'B'

🔵 GRADE 6 MENS: WSBC 2-2 Navan

Summary: Grade 5 Mens ‘B' and Grade 6 Mens both drew 2-2 with Boyne and WSBC. Grade 4 Mens lost 3-1 to Ardee 'A'.

🏸 Thursday, 23rd January 2025

🟢 SENIOR MIXED: Navan 'B' 5-7 Navan 'C' - CUP

🔵 GRADE 4 MENS: Navan 4-0 Julianstown

🟣 GRADE 4 LADIES: WSBC 2-2 Navan 'B'

🟢 GRADE 5 MIXED: Ardee 7-1 Navan 'A' - CUP

🟢 GRADE 6 MIXED: Navan 2-2 Ardee

Summary: Grade 4 Mens won 4-0 against Julianstown. Grade 4 Ladies 'B' drew 2-2 with WSBC. Grade 5 Mixed 'A' lost 7-1 to Ardee in the Cup. Senior Mixed teams had a close match with Navan 'C' winning. Grade 6 Mixed team drew 2-2 with Ardee.

Not all results were in our favor, but our teams showed great effort. Well done to everyone for representing Navan! Let's prepare for the next week.
