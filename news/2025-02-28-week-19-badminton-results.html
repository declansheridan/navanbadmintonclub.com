Week 19 Navan Badminton Results

Another week of intense badminton action! Here are the highlights.

🏸 Monday, 24th February 2025

  • 🔵 GRADE 3 MENS: Navan 'A' 3-1 Ardee
  • 🔵 GRADE 4 MENS: Navan 2-2 Bailieboro
  • 🟣 GRADE 4 LADIES: Navan 'B' 3-1 WSBC
  • 🟢 GRADE 7 MIXED: Drumree 0-4 Navan 'A'
  • 🟢 GRADE 7 MIXED: Longwood 7-2 Navan 'B' - CUP

Quick Review: Grade 3 Mens won 3-1 against Ardee. Grade 4 Mens had a 2-2 draw with Bailieboro. Grade 4 Ladies 'B' won 3-1 against WSBC. Grade 7 Mixed 'A' won 4-0 over Drumree. Grade 7 Mixed 'B' lost 7-2 to Longwood in the Cup.

🏸 Tuesday, 25th February 2025

  • 🟣 GRADE 7 LADIES: Trim 5-7 Navan - CUP

Quick Review: Grade 7 Ladies won 7-5 against Trim in the Cup.

🏸 Wednesday, 26th February 2025

  • 🟢 GRADE 3 MIXED: Navan 'B' 1-3 Turin
  • 🔵 GRADE 5 MENS: Boyne 1-7 Navan 'B' - CUP
  • 🔵 GRADE 6 MENS: Navan 2-2 Ardee

Quick Review: Grade 3 Mixed 'B' lost 1-3 to Turin. Grade 5 Mens 'B' won 7-1 over Boyne in the Cup. Grade 6 Mens drew 2-2 with Ardee.

🏸 Thursday, 27th February 2025

  • 🔵 SENIOR MENS: Kildalkey 8-0 Navan 'B' - CUP
  • 🔵 GRADE 5 MENS: Bailieboro 1-3 Navan 'A'
  • 🟣 GRADE 5 LADIES: Navan 2-2 Athboy/Kells
  • 🟣 GRADE 7 LADIES: Athboy/Kells 1-3 Navan

Quick Review: Senior Mens 'B' lost 0-8 to Kildalkey in the Cup. Grade 5 Mens 'A' won 3-1 over Bailieboro. Grade 5 Ladies drew 2-2 with Athboy/Kells. Grade 7 Ladies won 3-1 over Athboy/Kells.

Congrats to all players! Looking forward to next week's matches.
