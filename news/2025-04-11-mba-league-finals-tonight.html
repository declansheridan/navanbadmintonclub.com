No Thursday Club Night tonight, folks - no courts available. But it's for a good reason! It's the first night of the MBA Men's and Ladies League Finals.

Matches:

  • Grade 2 Men's
  • Grade 3 Ladies
  • Grade 6 Ladies

Big cheer for our Grade 3 Ladies in their final tonight. Drop by Claremont Stadium if you can - bring your energy and support!

Also Competing:

  • Grade 7 Mixed 'C' are in their Cup Semi-Final in Longwood. Good luck!
  • Grade 2 Inter League Team face Kilcullen. We're rooting for you!

While our usual Thursday activities are on hold, the club spirit is strong. We'll return next week for more club night fun!

Let's go, team! 💪🥳
