Leinster CAC Inter County Preliminaries: Navan Badminton Club Represented

Authors
  • Name
    NBC
    Twitter
    @NavanBC

Players from across the county will compete, and we're proud to have Navan Badminton Club in each of the five Meath panels for Grade 2, Grade 4, and Grade 6.

It's awesome for our members to join this event. Whether it's your first time or you're experienced, enjoy, support your teammates, and have fun!

Good luck to all Navan and Meath players across Leinster. We'll be cheering for you!
