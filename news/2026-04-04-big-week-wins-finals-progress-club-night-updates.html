Big Week of Wins, Finals Progress & Key Club Night Updates

Authors

Last Week:

A brilliant finish to the regular season with wins for:

  • Grade 3 Ladies ‘A’
  • Grade 7 Ladies
  • Grade 2 Ladies ‘A’

Inter League Highlight

Huge congratulations to our Grade 7 Inter League team with a 4–0 Quarter-Final win — booked their place in Terenure for Inter League Finals Day. Superb achievement!

League Quarter-Finals

  • Grade 4 Men battled hard and came through against Mullagh
  • Grade 7 Ladies put in a strong performance vs Drumree, but just missed out.
  • Grade 6 Men were edged out in a 1-point thriller — agonisingly close. Massive credit for a great season.

Cup Semi-Final

  • Grade 2 Mixed ‘A’ won on points vs Athboy/Kells ‘A’ — fantastic result and into the Final!
  • Grade 4 Men drew 6–6 in a huge effort but lost out on points and just missed the Final.

Next Week:

We’ve got 5 League Semi-Finals coming up!

And a special shoutout to Grade 7 Men ‘B’ — Cup Final on Tuesday vs Mullagh in Kildalkey. The whole club is behind you!

Best of luck to all our teams — leave it all on the court!

IMPORTANT THIS WEEK (Club Nights)

  • No Monday Club Night → Public Holiday
  • No Wednesday Club Night → MBA Semi Finals
  • Only Club Night next week: Thursday 9th April (9pm–11pm)
