Week 25 Results Recap

Authors
  • Name
    NBC
    Twitter
    @NavanBC

What. A. Week. We've had lots of exciting matches, close games, comebacks, and some wins! From Interleague clashes to League and Cup semi-finals or finals, our teams gave their all.

🏸 Monday, 7th April 2025

  • 🔵 GRADE 3 MENS: Navan 'A' 2-1 (Sets: 5-2) Mullingar - LGE SF
  • 🟢 GRADE 4 INTERLEAGUE: Navan 2-4 Portlaoise - QF
  • 🔵 GRADE 5 MENS: Mullingar 3-1 (Sets: 6-2) Navan 'B' - LGE SF

Quick Review: Grade 3 Mens 'A' won against Mullingar to reach the league final. Grade 4 Interleague had a tough match, losing 4-2 to Portlaoise. Grade 5 Mens 'B' faced a strong Mullingar side and lost in the semi-final. Good effort by everyone!

🏸 Tuesday, 8th April 2025

  • 🔵 GRADE 2 MENS: Navan 'B' 2-7 Dundalk - CUP QF
  • 🟢 GRADE 3 INTERLEAGUE: Navan 4-0 Turin - QF
  • 🟣 GRADE 7 LADIES: WSBC 0-3 (Sets: 1-6) Navan - LGE SF

Quick Review: Grade 2 Mens 'B' lost to Dundalk in the Cup QF. Grade 3 Interleague had a great 4-0 win over Turin. Grade 7 Ladies won 3-0 against WSBC and move to the finals. Well done!

🏸 Wednesday, 9th April 2025

  • 🔵 GRADE 4 MENS: Ardee 'B' 2-2 (Sets: 6-6, Points: 229-228) Navan - LGE SF
  • 🔵 GRADE 6 MENS: Longwood 2-0 (Sets: 5-1) Navan - LGE SF

Quick Review: Grade 4 Mens had a tight match with Ardee 'B', losing by just one point. Grade 6 Mens played hard but lost to Longwood. Tough luck but great effort!

🏸 Thursday, 10th April 2025

  • 🟢 GRADE 2 INTERLEAGUE: Kilcullen 3-4 Navan - QF
  • 🟣 GRADE 3 LADIES: Navan 2-1 (Sets: 6-3) Turin - LGE FINAL
  • 🟢 GRADE 7 MIXED: Longwood 7-2 Navan 'C' - CUP SF

Quick Review: Grade 2 Interleague came back from 3-1 down to win 4-3 against Kilcullen. Grade 3 Ladies won the league final against Turin. Grade 7 Mixed 'C' lost to Longwood in the cup semi. Great job everyone!

🏸 Friday, 11th April 2025

  • 🟢 GRADE 4 MIXED: Mullingar 3-7 Navan 'A' - CUP SF

Quick Review: Grade 4 Mixed 'A' beat Mullingar 7-3 in their Cup Semi-final. On to the final!

It was a busy week and we're enjoying every moment. Congrats to our G3 Ladies league champions, and good luck to all teams in the finals next week. Keep it up!
