Week 2 Results: Wins, Losses, and Learning Experiences

Authors
  • Name
    NBC
    Twitter
    @NavanBC

This week, we brought home some great wins - and a couple of humbling losses! But that's how we grow, one rally at a time. Here's how the week unfolded for our teams:

Monday, 21st October 2024

GRADE 3 LADIES: Navan 4-0 Boyne

Our Grade 3 Ladies didn't just win—they dominated! With a clean sweep, they sent Boyne home empty-handed. The court was practically on fire with our ladies' top-tier game, leaving no doubt about who ruled the net on Monday night.

Quick Review: Pure domination, all 4 games!

Tuesday, 22nd October 2024

GRADE 5 MENS: Mullingar 4-0 Navan A

Oof! Mullingar's Grade 5 Mens team proved to be a tough wall to break. Navan 'A' gave it their all, but sometimes the shots just don't land where you want them. We'll regroup, come back stronger, and put this match in the "learning experience" column!

Quick Review: Mullingar took the win this time, but we're not out yet!

Wednesday, 23rd October 2024

GRADE 7 LADIES: Mullagh 0-4 Navan

Now this was a highlight! Our Grade 7 Ladies absolutely brought the heat and shut Mullagh out completely! Four games out of four—they're on fire, and they're loving it! A round of applause for the ladies who truly made Wednesday theirs.

Quick Review: Complete shutout, and they loved every moment!

Thursday, 24th October 2024

GRADE 5 LADIES: Mullingar 4-0 Navan

Another tough outing in Mullingar. Our Grade 5 Ladies fought hard but couldn't crack Mullingar's defense. Sometimes the shuttle lands just outside the lines, but they took it all in stride with a battling performance. They'll be ready to bring their A-game next time.

Quick Review: Mullingar took the win, but our ladies fought to the end!

A solid week with two amazing wins and two losses that'll only fuel our fire! We're proud of every player who left it all on the court. With more matches ahead, expect more sparks to fly! 🔥
