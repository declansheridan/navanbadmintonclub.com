Week 10 Results: End of year 2024

Authors
  • Name
    NBC
    Twitter
    @NavanBC

That's it for 2024! Our players gave it their all in 7 matches this week. We had ups and downs on the courts—just how we like it! Here's the recap of our final week of the year:

🏸 Monday, 16th December 2024

  • 🟣 GRADE 3 LADIES: Navan 1-3 Turin
  • 🔵 GRADE 7 MENS: Navan 2-2 Kildalkey

Quick Review: Our Grade 3 Ladies had a tough match against Turin. The Grade 7 Men drew 2-2 with Kildalkey, keeping us on edge throughout.

🏸 Tuesday, 17th December 2024

  • 🟢 GRADE 4 MIXED: Mullingar 4-0 Navan 'B'

Quick Review: Our Grade 4 Mixed 'B' faced a strong Mullingar team. Despite the loss, they fought hard till the end.

🏸 Wednesday, 18th December 2024

  • 🟢 GRADE 3 MIXED: Navan 'A' 3-1 Ardee

Quick Review: Our Grade 3 Mixed 'A' shone with a 3-1 win against Ardee. A great midweek boost!

🏸 Thursday, 19th December 2024

  • 🟣 GRADE 4 LADIES: Navan 'B' 3-1 Skryne 'B'
  • 🟢 GRADE 5 MIXED: Bailieboro 4-0 Navan 'A'
  • 🟢 GRADE 7 MIXED: Navan 'B' 2-2 Mullagh 'A'

Quick Review: The Grade 4 Ladies 'B' team won 3-1 against Skryne 'B'. Our Grade 5 Mixed 'A' struggled against Bailieboro. The Grade 7 Mixed ended with a 2-2 draw against Mullagh 'A'.

That's a wrap for an amazing year of badminton. This week: 2 wins, 2 draws, and 3 losses. But it's the effort and teamwork that count.

Big thanks to all players for making this season special. Here's to more success in 2025. Enjoy the break, and see you next year!
