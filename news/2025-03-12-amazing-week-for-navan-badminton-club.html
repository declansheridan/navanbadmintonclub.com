Amazing Week for Navan Badminton Club

Authors
  • Name
    NBC
    Twitter
    @NavanBC

We're thrilled with 3 Mixed League Final wins!

🥇 Grade 7 Mixed 'A' started us off on Monday with a win over Trim at Claremont Stadium. Pauric, Qasim, Karen, and Dawn did great, pushing hard to win. Well done to all!

🥇 On Tuesday, our Senior Mixed 'A' team played in Kildalkey against Athboy/Kells. It was tough, but Willie, Paddy, Lynn, and Elaine stayed strong and won. Great job, team!

🥇 Also on Tuesday, our Grade 4 Mixed 'A' team won against Turin in Kildalkey. Dermot, Mark, Clare, and Marguerite fought hard and performed well.

Three finals, three wins! Navan did a clean sweep! Thanks to all players for their effort and to everyone who came to support us. Navan Badminton is on a high, and we're just getting started!
