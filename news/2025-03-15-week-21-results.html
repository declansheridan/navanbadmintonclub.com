Week 21 Results: League Champs and Playoff Clinchers

Authors
  • Name
    NBC
    Twitter
    @NavanBC

Three league champs, playoffs clinched, and intense matches—it doesn't get better than this!

🏸 Monday, 10th March 2025

🟢 GRADE 7 MIXED: Trim 1-2 (Sets: 3-5) Navan 'A' - LGE FINAL

Recap: Monday started strong with our Grade 7 Mixed 'A' edging out Trim to win the league! A close match, but they pulled through when needed!

🏸 Tuesday, 11th March 2025

🟢 SENIOR MIXED: Navan 'A' 3-0 (Sets: 6-1) Athboy/Kells - LGE FINAL

🟢 GRADE 4 MIXED: Navan 'A' 3-0 (Sets: 6-2) Turin - LGE FINAL

Recap: Tuesday saw our Senior Mixed 'A' and Grade 4 Mixed 'A' dominate, taking home two more league titles. Their season's effort paid off big time!

🏸 Wednesday, 12th March 2025

🔵 GRADE 2 MENS: Navan 0-4 Kildalkey

🔵 GRADE 4 MENS: Navan 3-1 Skryne

🔵 GRADE 5 MENS: Navan 'A' 1-7 Julianstown - CUP

Recap: Mixed results on Wednesday—Grade 4 Mens moved to the semis, Grade 2 Mens missed the final, and Grade 5 Mens 'A' faced a tough cup loss.

🏸 Thursday, 13th March 2025

🔵 SENIOR MENS: Turin 1-3 Navan

🔵 GRADE 5 MENS: Navan 'B' 3-1 WSBC

🔵 GRADE 6 MENS: Athboy/Kells 1-3 Navan

Recap: On Thursday, Grade 5 Mens 'B' made the playoffs, and Grade 6 Mens reached the semis. Senior Mens fought hard but fell to Turin.

A week full of wins, excitement, and memorable moments—congrats to all league champs and playoff teams!
